Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026 · Publisher: Tariq Almalki · Contact: support@souvenirtravel.app

In one paragraph

The Souvenir iPhone app does not have servers, does not have accounts, and does not collect anything about you. Everything the app records — your trips, the people you travelled with, your name and picture, copies of your trip photographs, and the destinations you save for your next journey — is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud, inside an area only your Apple Account can open. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone else.

Searching for your next destination

The destination data Souvenir searches — months, weather, visa requirements and nonstop flights — ships inside the app itself. Searching and filtering happen entirely on your device, and nothing about your search, your preferences or your passport nationality is sent anywhere.

What the app reads

Your photo library. With your permission, Souvenir reads the date and location recorded in your photographs so it can work out the journeys you took. This reading happens on your device. Your photographs are never sent to us or to any other company.

Your originals in Apple Photos are never modified, moved or deleted. Removing a photograph from a trip in Souvenir leaves the original untouched.

"Your private iCloud" means the private database of this app's iCloud container, inside your Apple Account. Apple provides it; the developer has no ability to read, export or browse its contents. It counts against your own iCloud storage.

Videos are not copied. They are played from Apple Photos where they already are.

What leaves your device, and to whom

Souvenir talks to three places, and never to ours:

  1. iCloud — to store your record and photograph copies in your own account, as described above.
  2. Apple Maps — when a place has no coordinates, its name (for example "Amsterdam, Netherlands") is sent to Apple to find where it is and to draw a map of it. No photograph and no personal information is sent with it.
  3. Wikivoyage and Wikimedia Commons — when a public picture of a city is shown (for a destination you are discovering, or a trip with no photographs of its own), the place name is sent to fetch its public photograph. Nothing about you is sent with it, and every borrowed picture is credited to its photographer inside the app.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDK, and no account to create.

Information about other people

If you add travelling companions, you type their names and may choose pictures of them. This is information about other people, and it is treated exactly like everything else here: kept in your own iCloud, readable only by you, and never sent to us. Please only add people who would be comfortable with that.

Children

Souvenir is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

  • On the device: delete the app.
  • In iCloud: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Souvenir → Delete Data. This removes everything the app has stored in your account, on every device.

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to make of us.

Souvenir on the web

At souvenirtravel.app you'll find Souvenir in your browser: search, compare, and wander between destinations completely freely — all of it runs in your browser, needs no account, and records nothing about you.

When you'd like to keep things — a destination that won your heart, a trip you're planning, your travel papers — you sign in with Google, and your keepsakes get one home that opens from any device. The idea is simple: what you save from your laptop today is on your phone tonight, and what you delete there doesn't come back to haunt you here.

What lives in that home? Only the useful little: your favourites and their months, your upcoming trips (destination and dates), your papers (the kind, the country, the expiry date — no photos, no passport numbers), and your preferences and passport nationality. From Google we receive only what you see on the page yourself: your name, email, and photo — your password stays with Google; we never see it and never want to.

This data is kept in a space that is yours alone, inside a secure Google database (Firebase) hosted in a data centre in Dammam — close to you. The access rules are written so that no other user can open your space, and we don't sell your data, share it, or use it for anything except making this feature work for you.

And whenever you wish to leave, the door is open, no questions asked: "Sign out" stops syncing at once, and a single email to support@souvenirtravel.app is enough for us to erase your space entirely and write back confirming it's gone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described in the app before they take effect.
