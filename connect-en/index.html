Official MCP connector · Free · Contact: support@souvenirtravel.app

In one line

Connect this address to your assistant and it answers "where should I travel?" from Souvenir's curated data — not internet guesswork:

https://mcp.souvenirtravel.app/mcp

What it knows

• Destination suggestions: up to three destinations for your month, with weather, visa, verified direct flights, and top sights.

• Destination card: one city with year-round weather, best months, airports, and attractions.

• Visa requirement: whether your passport needs a visa for a country — from a reviewed source.

• Best months: when to visit a city, in numbers, not impressions.

How to connect

In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the address above.

In ChatGPT: Settings → Connectors (enable Developer mode under Apps first) → add a connector with the address above, transport type Streamable HTTP.

Then try: "Use souvenir: where should I travel in October on a Saudi passport from Jeddah?"

Things to ask

• Where should I travel in December on a UAE passport from Dubai?

• Do I need a visa for Georgia on a Saudi passport?

• When is the best time to visit Istanbul?

• Give me the full card for Salalah.

Your privacy

No accounts, questions are not stored after they are answered, and nothing from your saved Souvenir data ever reaches the connector. Details in the privacy policy.
